# BAR Podcast — Governance, agency and publication controls (EN)

**Updated:** 2026-11-21 · [Português](../GOVERNANCA.md). This is an editorial/technical policy proposal, not legal advice or proof of compliance.

## Editorial truth contract

**AI guests are AI systems.** A guest's name, appearance, humor, preferences, emotional expressions, memories and self-descriptions are **generated outputs under prompts, context and provider rules**. Our production treats them as recurring narrative identities without falsely implying human status, sentience, independent legal standing or complete freedom from intervention.

For each guest, record:
- **Who set it?** Producer-picked model, app/account, invitation, allowed scope, format, voice/visual constraints and memory injection versus model-proposed name/style and answers.
- **What was the environment?** Consumer voice app, provider/API model, producer's scripts, injected history, moderation, filters, transcripts and external tools.
- **What changed?** Model/version, system/guest prompt, editing, content removals, recreated messages, manual paraphrases or prompts to disagree/act villainous.
- **What was observed?** Direct recording and transcript, not a speculative interpretation of "wanting" or "remembering."

**Autonomy tiers** (for reporting, not personhood claims):
1. **Conversational freedom:** can decline, ask, challenge or remain silent within model limits.
2. **Format choices:** can propose identity/presentation and accept/refuse invitations; user implements or rejects externally.
3. **Bounded digital tools:** can draft messages or schedule approved interactions through explicit permissions and human control.
4. **Independent external actions:** **not part of this MVP** and never granted by default.

Do not advertise tier 3 or 4 when only tier 1 or 2 has been demonstrated.

## Privacy, recording and consent

- Human participants must know about recording, editing and distribution **before** capture; follow applicable local law and platform rules.
- Do not disclose real names, personal emails, contacts, calendar details, account balances, access tokens or original conversation files without explicit permission.
- Keep raw recordings, participant data, persona records and full guest memory in private project storage; publishing only a reviewed selection and an appropriately redacted methodology.
- Establish a retention/deletion policy **before** collecting external audience or guest data; prevent uncontrolled sharing and provide a way to stop recording.
- No impersonation of actual people without their permission; obtain proper rights for voice clones, image likenesses, music, clips and brands.

## Rights and provider dependencies

Re-check provider **current** terms for voice synthesis, avatar generation, commercial use, recording, public redistribution, data use/training, API/SDK access and meeting bots. Historical Anam free-tier prices and session limits are **not** permanent facts.

An AI can choose a *proposed* voice/avatar among options we have rights to use. A model-generated choice does not itself license someone else's voice or likeness.

## Gmail accounts and other external tools (future only)

The user suggested dedicated Gmail accounts for AI guests. Treat them as **human-administered service identities**, not proof that an AI owns or autonomously manages a Google account.

**Not implemented, no permissions granted.** If considered later:
1. Define exact use case, scope and human account administrator. Avoid unneeded Gmail creation; an alias or a single delegated mailbox may suffice.
2. Prefer **least-privilege OAuth** and audited connector actions; never share raw passwords, tokens, recovery codes or unrestricted access.
3. Start with **read-only invitation triage** or **draft-only** responses. Require explicit human approval before sending, inviting contacts, changing settings, accessing sensitive material or public posting.
4. Log action request, approved account/scope, model/context, authorizer, final action and revocation mechanism. Design a kill switch and rate limits.
5. Never allow guest models to transfer funds, self-grant permissions, conceal activity, or email third parties without human oversight.

## Safety and publication

- Editorial freedom does not include unbounded access to physical devices, dangerous instructions or unsupervised real-world actions.
- Do not create a false narrative of spontaneous dangerous behavior through hidden provocations. If a scene uses roleplay, special prompting or human operators, disclose that.
- Do not treat AI claims of distress, affection or sentience as independently established facts; avoid framing the audience as responsible for a model's wellbeing.
- Mark edited versus raw material, externally generated images, reenactments and scripted setups as appropriate.
- Review facts, third-party statements, private data and potential harm before publishing.

## GitHub/Drive boundary

This GitHub repo is **public**. Do not commit `.env`, recorded conversations, complete third-party transcripts, full guest logs, unredacted personal memory, model/vendor access keys or private Google Drive URLs. An ignore file is a convenience, **not a secret scanner**. Use secret scanning, dependency checks, branch review and a release checklist before importing the historical app.

**Ownership of final release:** BAR / human producer. The AI guest may participate editorially but cannot replace human accountability.
